# Anatomi & Batas Karakter Seksi LinkedIn

Format heading chunk: `## [bahasa|seksi] Judul` — diparse oleh
`scripts/ingest-branding-kb.mjs`. Satu heading = satu chunk embedding.

## [id|experience] Anatomi seksi Experience

Seksi Experience LinkedIn terdiri dari: Title (maks 100 karakter), Company/Organization (maks 100), Start date – End date, Location (opsional), dan Description (maks 2000 karakter). Untuk pengalaman organisasi mahasiswa, tulis peran sebagai Title (mis. "Ketua Divisi Humas") dan nama organisasi sebagai Company. Description diisi 3–5 bullet pencapaian, bukan daftar tugas. Baris pertama description adalah yang terlihat sebelum tombol "see more" — letakkan pencapaian terkuat di sana.

## [en|experience] Anatomy of the Experience section

A LinkedIn Experience entry has: Title (max 100 characters), Company/Organization (max 100), Start–End dates, optional Location, and a Description (max 2000 characters). For student organization roles, put the role as Title (e.g. "Head of Public Relations Division") and the organization as Company. Write 3–5 achievement bullets, not a duty list. The first line shows before the "see more" fold — lead with your strongest, most quantified achievement.

## [id|certification] Anatomi seksi Licenses & Certifications

Field yang tersedia: Name (nama sertifikasi persis seperti di sertifikat), Issuing organization, Issue date, Expiration date (opsional), Credential ID (opsional), Credential URL (opsional). Jangan menambahkan kata "certified" berlebihan di Name — tulis nama resmi program. Cantumkan Credential ID dan URL bila ada karena meningkatkan kredibilitas dan bisa diverifikasi perekrut.

## [en|certification] Anatomy of Licenses & Certifications

Available fields: Name (exactly as printed on the certificate), Issuing organization, Issue date, optional Expiration date, optional Credential ID, optional Credential URL. Use the program's official name. Always fill Credential ID and URL when available — recruiters can verify them, which raises trust.

## [id|award] Anatomi seksi Honors & Awards

Field: Title (nama penghargaan), Associated with (pilih pendidikan/pekerjaan terkait), Issuer (penyelenggara), Issue date, Description (maks 2000 karakter). Title ditulis lengkap dengan peringkat dan skala, mis. "Juara 2 — Hackathon Nasional XYZ 2026". Description menjelaskan konteks kompetisi (jumlah peserta/tim, skala), kontribusi spesifik Anda, dan hasilnya dalam 2–4 kalimat atau bullet.

## [en|award] Anatomy of Honors & Awards

Fields: Title (award name), Associated with, Issuer, Issue date, Description (max 2000 characters). Write the Title with rank and scale, e.g. "2nd Place — XYZ National Hackathon 2026". In the Description, give the competition context (number of teams, scale), your specific contribution, and the outcome in 2–4 sentences or bullets.

## [id|volunteering] Anatomi seksi Volunteer Experience

Field: Role, Organization, Cause (pilihan tetap LinkedIn, mis. Education, Environment), tanggal, dan Description. Seksi ini menyambung jam kegiatan sosial (mis. syarat BINUS 30 jam community service). Cantumkan total jam dan dampak konkret: "±40 jam mengajar literasi digital untuk 60 siswa SD". Perekrut menilai konsistensi kontribusi sosial, bukan sekadar kehadiran.

## [en|volunteering] Anatomy of Volunteer Experience

Fields: Role, Organization, Cause (fixed LinkedIn options such as Education, Environment), dates, Description. This section maps to community-service hours (e.g. the 30-hour BINUS requirement). State total hours and concrete impact: "~40 hours teaching digital literacy to 60 primary-school students". Recruiters value sustained contribution over one-off attendance.

## [id|umum] Prinsip umum profil mahasiswa

Gunakan sudut pandang orang pertama tanpa kata "saya" berulang. Konsisten satu bahasa per entri. Angka selalu ditulis sebagai digit ("60 peserta", bukan "enam puluh"). Hindari jargon internal kampus yang tidak dipahami perekrut luar; jelaskan singkatan pada penyebutan pertama. Setiap entri harus bisa dipahami orang yang tidak mengenal organisasi/lombanya.

## [en|umum] General principles for student profiles

Write in implicit first person without repeating "I". Keep one language per entry. Always write numbers as digits ("60 participants", not "sixty"). Avoid campus-internal jargon; expand abbreviations on first mention. Every entry must make sense to a recruiter who has never heard of the organization or competition.
